# Slice 05 ink shots

Rendered with `MODE=ink node apps/macos/tests/overlay-shots.mjs`: a stroke on light and dark,
a stroke mid-fade, two pins on light and dark, and the take after undoing pin 2. Defaults
chosen pending the user's review: ink #ff3b30, 5 pt strokes with a white edge and soft shadow so
they hold on red, busy or light content, pins 32 pt across with a white edge, strokes fade
over 1.2 s after ⇧ is released, pins stay until undone. Two unprimed critiques; the second
asked for the edges and larger pins.
